==Area of Triangles==

The area of a triangle is $A= \frac{1}{2}\times base\times height$. If we know the lengths of 2 sides and the included angle of a triangle .then we can find the area. What if it is not right angle triangle?

Case 1 acute angle.

![[Pasted image 20260607095101.png|202]]

In this case we need to find the height which is $h$. The associated trigonometric function with $\theta$ is $\cos$ and $\tan$. Thus,

$$
\begin{align}
h&=b\sin \theta \\
h&= x\tan \theta
\end{align}
$$
where $x$ is a part of $a$. To make it clean, we use the first one. Thus, the area is

$$
\frac{1}{2}\times ab\sin \theta
$$
Case 2: the angle is obtuse
![[Pasted image 20260607095858.png]]

It is like a parallelogram. Why? it is a horizontal shear of a right angle triangle. That's why the height is being defined as the vertical ray connected to the shear vertical ray.

Thus, the height is again

$$
h=b\sin (180^\circ -\theta)=b\sin \theta
$$

Note that the $180^\circ-\theta$ is the reference angle for $\theta$. Why in this case $\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin \theta$, because we are considering the area, so the sign of trigonometric function must be positive. (The length is a magnitude). Thus

$$
A=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin \theta
$$

Thus,

> [!info] Area of a triangle
The area $A$ of a triangle with sides of lengths $a$ and $b$ and with included angle $\theta$ is
>
>$$
A=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin \theta
$$
