Solution Curves & Phase Line dy/dx = y² - 2y

Visualizing equilibrium lines, inflection line y = 1, and monotonicity

y = 2 (Unstable Repeller) y = 0 (Stable Attractor)
Top Region (y > 2)

y' > 0 (increasing) & y'' > 0 (concave up ⌣). Curves peel away from $y = 2$ and shoot to $+\infty$.

Middle Region (0 < y < 2)

y' < 0 (decreasing S-curve). Curves leave $y = 2$ (concave down ⌢), cross inflection line y = 1, and level off at $y = 0$ (concave up ⌣).

Bottom Region (y < 0)

y' > 0 (increasing) & y'' < 0 (concave down ⌢). Curves rise from $-\infty$ and gently level off against $y = 0$.
