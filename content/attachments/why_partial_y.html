Why ∂f/∂y and NOT ∂f/∂x? The Vertical Slice
At any fixed x, Δx = 0. The gap between curves is purely vertical (Δy)!
Position x: 1.00
Vertical Gap Δy: 0.20
On the Vertical Slice (Fixed x) Δx = 0
Slope at y₁: 0.00
Slope at y₂ (y₁ + Δy): 0.20
Slope Difference: 0.20
Ratio: Δ(Slope) / Δy 1.00

This ratio is definitionally ∂f/∂y!
