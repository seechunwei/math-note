### How to define learning?
Is learning related to a action like, study book, attend lecture, do tutorial or sit in front of your study table?

This is the outer description of the action, what happen inside one's mind? 2 different person can go to lecture with same amount of time but has a huge difference in their mathematic ability.

Maybe one focus in class, make note, try to find the connection of each chunk of knowledge while another one just passively listen what lecturer is talking.

So learning is flexible and yet it it can be deceptive in the sense of not efficient.

So learning is the process to enhance your mathematic understanding , when you see some definition, theorem or a particular solution for problem, you have the ability to abstract its core concept, stripping the detail, it is an encapsulation that make it become transportable other field.

The 3 step to do encapsulation
1) Give it a name
2) Explain its core concept
3) Propose 3 non trivial example in other field

Example:

When we do this it can become a mental shortcut where everything like the technical detail is being packed to become a module. It is like a function when some condition is being trigger, then we call the function and it map to a output.

But the 3 step for encapsulation is a very high standard process,

### The 5-Step Training Loop
1) The Problem: Find a mathematic question, how to define a good question? 
 - We cannot solve the problem or this problem reveal a lack of knowledge or boundary of existing knowledge/ expose specific knowledge gaps. But cannot too difficult and cannot too easy as well.
 
For example: How to know if this system of linear equation always have a solution? (This question expose us to the knowledge of  vector space (pivot))

2) The Attempt: Engage in a rigorous, independent struggle to find a solution.
Goal: Expose the logic's breaking point and get brain ready for acquisition 

3) The specific question: Convert a vague failure into a technical inquiry
 _Primary Objective:_ Pinpoint the exact definition, transition, or property that caused the cognitive stall.

4) The solution/Feedback: Seek for help from AI or documentation or instructors
it a process of actively extract the knowledge

5) The verification/Review: Validate the solution's universality and compress the insight.
Abstract the core idea and make sure it is logically correct and you can find other example that have the same spirit(pattern) with the question. Write a proof to verify it, transfer testing.
