
What is variability?
- Variability of a data set refers to the spread, scatter or dispersion of the scores.
- A distribution of scores is said to be highly variable if the scores differ greatly from one another.

#### Variance and Standard Deviation

The value of the standard deviation tells how closely the values of a data set are clustered around the mean.
- Lower standard deviation means data set spread over a relatively smaller range around the mean
- Larger standard deviation values of that data set are spread over a relatively larger range around the mean.
