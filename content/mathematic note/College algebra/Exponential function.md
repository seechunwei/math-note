Defining an Exponential Function

Exponential growth refers to an increase based on a constant multiplicative rate of change over equal increments of time, that is, a percent increase of the original amount over time. (change of magnitude)

Exponential decay refers to a decrease based on a constant multiplicative rate of change over equal increments of time, that is, a percent decrease of the original amount over time.

The general form of the exponential function is f(x)=$ab^{x}$, where a is any nonzero number, b is a positive real number not equal to 1.

Why $b>0$ and $b\neq 1$, because if $b=1$ or $b=0$, then the graph is a horizontal line. if $b< 0$, let say -1. Thus, the graph is no more continuous.

If b > 1, the function grows at a rate proportional to its size.
• If 0 <b < 1, the function decays at a rate proportional to its size.

> [!info] The number $e$
The natural number $e$ represent the irrational number 
>$$\left( 1+\frac{1}{n} \right)^{n}, \text{ as n increase without bound}$$

Notice that this is the compound interest formula when we when we save RM1 for one year and the nominal rate is $100$% which compound $n$ times per year.

### Investigating Continuous growth


Exponential models that use e as the base are called continuous growth or decay models.

$$
A(t)=ae^{rt}
$$
a is initial value
$r$ is the continuous growth rate per unit time
and $t$ is the elapsed time
If r> 0, then the formula represents continuous growth. If r <0, then the formula represents continuous decay.

### Graphing Transformations of Exponential Function
Basic function
$$
g(x)=ab^{x}
$$

1) Vertical shift
$$
f(x)=ab^{x}+c
$$
is a new function from $g(x)=ab^{x}$ under vertical shift of $c$ units up.

Horizontal shift
$$
f(x)=ab^{x-c}
$$
is a new function perform horizontal shift $c$ unit to right.(opposite direction) Why?

Let $f(x)=y$
$$
\begin{align}
y&=ab^{x-c} \\
\frac{y}{a}&=\frac{b^{x}}{b^{c}} \\
\frac{yb^{c}}{a}&=b^{x} \\
x&=\log _{b} \frac{yb^{c}}{a} \text{ ,b>1}\\
x&=\log_{b} \frac{y}{a}+c
\end{align}
$$
