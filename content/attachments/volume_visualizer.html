3. 3D Solid Volume (3 Vectors: u, v, w) Solid 3D space
Volume = (Base Area) × (Height h)
= ||u × v|| × (w · (u × v) / ||u × v||)
= w · (u × v) = det [u   v   w]
Total 3D Volume: 10.000

📐 Why ||u|| ||v|| sin θ is AREA, not volume:

You only have two vectors u and v. Two vectors span a flat 2D sheet (a parallelogram) like a sheet of paper floating in space. A sheet of paper has length and width, but zero thickness. Its volume is 0! Its 2D surface area is base × height = ||u|| (||v|| sin θ).

📦 How VOLUME is created:

To get 3D volume, you must introduce a third vector w pointing out of the plane. This stretches the flat parallelogram into a 3D box (parallelepiped). Volume is always:
Volume = (Base Area) × (Perpendicular Height) = det [u   v   w]
