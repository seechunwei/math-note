For example why if $r_{2}\in Span\{ r_{1} \}$, then $A$ is not invertible.

It is because the system $\begin{bmatrix}A & I\end{bmatrix}$ is inconsistent. When we try to row reduce $A$ there exist one row of all zero at the coefficient matrix. Thus, $A$ is not row equivalent to $I$. Thus, $A$ is not invertible. 

This is different from column, $c_{i}\in Span\{ c_{k} \}$ for $i\neq j$. Thus

$$
c_{i}=bc_{k}
$$
for some $b \in \mathbb{R}-\{ 0 \}$. Why $b$ cannot be zero because we suppose $c_{2}$ is not zero vector.


Suppose WLOG $i>k$ then


$$
\begin{align}
c_{i}&=0c_{1}+0c_{2}+\dots+bc_{k}+\dots+0c_{i-1} \\
\end{align}
$$

Thus, the set $\{ c_{1},c_{2},\dots,c_{i} \}$ is linearly dependent. Hence,

$$
\{ c_{1},c_{2},\dots c_{k},\dots c_{n} \}
$$
is also linearly dependent as

$$
0c_{1}+\dots+bc_{k}+\dots+(-c_{i})+\dots 0c_{n}=0
$$

Hence the column of $A$ is linearly dependent, that is $Ax=0$ has non trivial solution(by definition)
