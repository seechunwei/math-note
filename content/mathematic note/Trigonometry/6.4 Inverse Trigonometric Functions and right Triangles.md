
For a function to have an inverse, it must be one-to-one and onto. Since the trigonometric function is not one-to-one, it follow that we need to restrict the domain.

Let's consider the $\sin$ function. Since $\sin \theta= \frac{y}{r}$, and $y$ does not repeat in interval $\left[ -\frac{\pi}{2} , \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$. Thus we restrict the domain of $\sin \theta$ with the interval as domain.

It is the same for $\cos$ and $\tan$.
