## 📚 Curriculum & Knowledge Structure

### 🧠 Real Analysis & Calculus (`Mathematic learning/`)
* **Foundations of Numbers:** Basic properties, real numbers, Dedekind cuts & Archimedean properties
* **Functions & Graphs:** Functional architecture, curves, conic sections (parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas)
* **Problem Toolboxes:**
  * The Critical Point Partitioning Principle
  * Polarization & Lagrange Conjugates
  * Telescoping Sums & Consecutive Differences
  * Local Proximity Bounding & Detour Principles

### 📐 Linear Algebra (`mathematic note/linear algebra 111` & `strang book`)
* **Gilbert Strang Curriculum:**
  * Gaussian elimination, $LU$ and $LDU$ factorization
  * Vector spaces, linear independence, basis, and dimension
  * Four fundamental subspaces & the Rank-Nullity Theorem
  * Determinants, hypervolume, eigenvalues, and eigenvectors
  * Inner product spaces, Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization, and least squares

### 🔢 Discrete Mathematics & Mathematical Proofs (`mathematic note/math 100`)
* **Logic & Quantifiers:** Compound statements, conditional logic, multi-quantifier statements
* **Methods of Proof:** Direct proof, contraposition, contradiction, mathematical induction
* **Elementary Number Theory:** Well-Ordering Principle (WOP), Euclid's Lemma, Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (FTA), Bézout's Identity, and Modular Arithmetic

### 📊 Probability & Statistics (`mathematic note/math 161`)
* Descriptive statistics & variability
* Discrete & continuous random variables
* Sampling distributions & Central Limit Theorem
* Inferential statistics & hypothesis testing (Chi-square, two-population tests)
