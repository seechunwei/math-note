Top Region (y > 3)

y' > 0 (increasing) & y'' > 0 (concave up ⌣). Solutions peel away from $y = 3$ and shoot to $+\infty$.

Middle Region (-2 < y < 3)

y' < 0 (decreasing S-curve). Curves leave $y = 3$ (concave down ⌢), cross y = 1/2 (inflection), and level off at $y = -2$ (concave up ⌣).

Bottom Region (y < -2)

y' > 0 (increasing) & y'' < 0 (concave down ⌢). Solutions rise from $-\infty$ and gently level off against $y = -2$.
