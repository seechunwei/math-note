Key Physical Insight:
• At switch turn-on: Water rushes to accelerate, so di/dt is large. The heavy wheel absorbs almost all pressure (VL ≈ E), so current rises gradually.
• At steady state: Acceleration drops to zero (di/dt = 0). The wheel spins freely with zero back-pressure (VL = 0), leaving all voltage drop across the resistor (Ri = E).
