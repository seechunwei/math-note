### Angle measure

Definition of Radian Measure

If a circle of radius 1 is drawn with the vertex of an angle $\theta$ at its center, then the measure of $\theta$ in radians (abbreviated rad) is the length of the arc that subtends the angle.

Which means that the measurement of angle and arc are the same in different unit. And they are proportional to each other.

For example an arc that subtends the angle 2 rad has the length of 2 unit. 

The circumference of the circle of radius 1 is $2\pi$, so a complete revolution has measure $2\pi$ rad, a straight angle has measure $\pi$ rad, and a right angle has measure $\frac{\pi}{2}$ rad.

==The relationship between degrees and radians==
$$
\begin{align}
360 ^\circ  &=2\pi \text{ rad } \\
1rad&= \frac{180^\circ }{\pi}
\end{align}
$$
$$
1^\circ =\frac{\pi}{180}rad
$$


==Angle in Standard Position==
An angle is in standard position when it is drawn in the xy-plane with its vertex at the origin and its initial side on the positive x-axis.

==Length of circular arc==

In a circle of radius $r$ the length $s$ of an arc that subtend a central angle of $\theta$ radians is 
$$
s=r\theta
$$

Why? because $2\pi$ is the circumstance of circle when $r=1$, since angle and length of arc are proportional, thus $s=r\theta$

Solving for $\theta$, we get 

$$
\theta= \frac{s}{r}
$$

\end{align}
