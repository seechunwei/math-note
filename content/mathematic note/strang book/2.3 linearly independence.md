==Definition==
A basis for $V$ is a sequence of vectors having  two properties at once:
1. The vectors are linearly independent (not too many vectors).
2. They span the space V (not too few vectors).

It means that every vector in the space is a combination of the basis vectors, because they span. It also means that the combination is unique because they are linearly independent.^123 ^9d6540

Suppose $v=a_{1}v_{1}+\dots+a_{n}v_{n}$ and $v=b_{1}v_{1}+\dots+b_{n}v_{n}$. Then,

$$
\sum(ai-bi)v_{i}=0
$$

It means that there is unique solution for every $b$.

A vector space has infinitely many different bases. Whenever a square matrix is invertible, its columns are independent—and they are a basis for $\mathbb{R}^{n}$.

$$
U=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 3 & 3 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 3 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

For this example the column space of $U$ span a plane in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$. Thus, The columns that contain pivots(in this case the first and third, which correspond to the basic variables) are a basis for the column space.

To summarize: The columns of any matrix span its column space. If they are independent, they are a basis for the column space


### Dimension of a Vector Space

==Theorem==
Any two bases for a vector space V contain the same number of vectors. This number, which is shared by all bases and expresses the number of“ degrees of freedom” of the space, is the dimension of V.


==Theorem==
if $v_{1},\dots v_{m}$ and $w_{1},\dots,w_{n}$ are both bases for the same vector space, then $m=n$. The number of vectors in the same.
