38) Bisectors of two supplementary angles are perpendicular to each other.

Proof: The bisectors in a line that divide the angle into 2. Suppose 2 arbitrary supplementary angles $\angle AOB$ and $\angle BOC$. Thus, by definition
$$
\begin{align}
\angle AOB+\angle BOC=180^\circ  \\
 \frac{\angle AOB}{2}+\frac{\angle  BOC}{2}=90^\circ 
\end{align}
$$
Notice that the angle form by 2 bisectors is $\frac{\angle AOB}{2}+ \frac{\angle BOC}{2}=90^\circ$. Thus 4 angles form by the continuation of the bisectors are $90^\circ$. (actually we just need to show one of them is $90^\circ$) Thus, they are perpendicular to each other.

39) Bisectors of two vertical angles are continuations of each other. 

Proof:
Suppose 2 arbitrary vertical angles $\angle AOB$ and $\angle COD$ and their supplementary angles $\angle COA$ and $\angle BOD$. 

Notice that bisectors of two supplementary angles are perpendicular to each other. Thus, suppose a bisector $OA_{1}$ for $\angle AOB$, a bisector $OB_{1}$ for $\angle BOD$ and a bisector $OC_{1}$ for $\angle COD$ . Since, $\angle AOB$ and $\angle BOD$ are supplementary angle, it follow that the the $OA_{1}$ and $OB_{1}$ are perpendicular to each other, thus $\angle A_{1}OB_{1}=90^\circ$

The same argument apply to $\angle COD$ and $\angle BOD$. Since they are supplementary angle, it follow  that $OB_{1}$ and $OC_{1}$ are perpendicular to each other. Thus, $\angle B_{1}OC_{1}=90^\circ$

Therefore the big angle construct by 2 bisectors of vertical angle which is $\angle A_{1}OC_{1}=\angle A_{1}OB_{1}+\angle B_{1}OC_{1}=180^\circ$. Thus, it follow that $A_{1}OC_{1}$ is a straight line. Therefore, $OA_{1}$ and $OC_{1}$ are continuations of each other.
