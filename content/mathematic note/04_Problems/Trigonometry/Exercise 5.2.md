==Remark==
Notice that usually $\tan t<\csc t$ and $\tan t<\sec t$. Thus when express $\tan t$ in term of  $\sec t$, it is $\sec ^{2}t-1$.

While $\sec t$ in term of $\tan t$ is $1+\tan ^{2}t$.

look at the **magnitudes (absolute values)** of $\sec t$ and $\tan t$.

Because $\sec^2 t = \tan^2 t + 1$, **$\sec t$ is always the "larger" function in terms of absolute size** ($|\sec t| \ge 1$, whereas $|\tan t|$ can be anything from $0$ to infinity).
