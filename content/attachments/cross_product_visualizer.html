💡 The Core Intuition: A tilted 2D parallelogram in 3D space casts three flat shadows onto the coordinate planes. The area of each shadow is computed by a standard 2D determinant. By the 3D generalization of Pythagoras' theorem (De Gua's theorem), the square of the total 3D area equals the sum of the squares of the three shadow areas: Area² = (Shadow_yz)² + (Shadow_xz)² + (Shadow_xy)².

The cross product vector u × v stores each of these three shadow areas into the corresponding perpendicular axis direction (i, j, k). Its direction automatically aligns with the true 3D surface normal!
